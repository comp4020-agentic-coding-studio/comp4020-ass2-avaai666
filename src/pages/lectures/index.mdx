Ten lectures across twelve weeks. Week 6 is field week and week 12 is the
exhibit, and neither of them needs a lecture more than it needs the room.

Each lecture introduces one class of machine and the shape of error it leaves
behind. They are in order on purpose: the errors get harder to see as the
semester goes on, and by week 11 they are almost gone.
