## The contract

who promised what, to whom, and how you establish that they did

---

## Three verification levels

- **primary** — Recorded from the artefact, or from a report that reproduces it.
- **secondary** — Reported by others. This course has not seen the artefact itself.
- **apocryphal** — Widely repeated. No primary source has been found.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> `apocryphal` is a finding rather than a failure

---

## What a record has to contain

- what the artefact said
- where and when it was recorded
- how confident you are that it exists as described
- what you think produced it
