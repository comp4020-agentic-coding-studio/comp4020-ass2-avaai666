## An error that does not announce itself

A statistical system, starved of input, returns something broken.

A neural system, starved of input, returns a sentence.

It has no other mode. Sentences are what it is for.

> Given nothing, it produced scripture.

## 2018

People fed repeated nonsense syllables into low-resource language pairs.

What came back was fluent apocalyptic prose.

The explanation is not mysterious. For several of those languages, the largest
available parallel text was scripture.

## The output was a portrait of the training set

This is the most legible moment in the whole course.

Nobody published the corpus. The model described it anyway, under conditions
where it had nothing else to say.

---

## What that means for the method

The reconstruction still works — but the evidence has moved.

- **before** — the error tells you about the table
- **now** — the *shape of the invention* tells you about the distribution

You are no longer reading a mistake. You are reading a default.

---

## And what it costs

The error was a free, involuntary disclosure.

It arrived on every sign, in public, without anybody's permission.

Week 11 is about what happens when it stops arriving.

---

## At the bench on Wednesday

1. pick a pair with little parallel text
2. feed it input with no content
3. **log the system, the direction and the date**
4. repeat one input four times

Step 3 is not admin. In six months your log is the only evidence this behaviour
existed.
