For nine weeks you have found errors. Today you manufacture them.

## The protocol

Take one sentence. Translate it into a second language, then back. Then out
again, then back. Log every hop with the system and the date.

Somewhere between the fourth and the twelfth iteration the text usually stops
changing. Record which iteration, and what it settled into.

## The reading

A fixed point is not a translation of anything. It is the sentence the system
considers its own best output for its own previous output, which makes it a
portrait of the system's preferences rather than of your original.

Say what that portrait shows: register, sentence length, the words it will not
give up.

## Afterwards

Compare fixed points across the room. Two people who started from very different
sentences and arrived somewhere similar have found something about the system.
Two who did not have found something about their sentences.
