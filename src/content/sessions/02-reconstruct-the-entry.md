You are given three signs. You do not get the dictionary. Write the entry.

## The exercise

Work in pairs. Each pair gets outputs from the same source and reconstructs the
lookup that produced them: the key, the senses under it, and the order they must
have been in.

Then swap with another pair and try to break their entry with a fourth output
they have not seen.

## What we are looking for

Not the right answer — there is no key to check against, which is the condition
this whole course works under. What the bench is looking for is whether you can
separate the two kinds of sentence in your write-up: *the sign says X* and *the
table must have contained Y*. The first is the artefact. The second is yours,
and it is the part that can be wrong.

## Afterwards

Keep the entry. In week 4 you will do the same exercise against a system that
has no entries at all, and the contrast is the lesson.
