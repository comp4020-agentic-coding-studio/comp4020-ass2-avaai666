Bring something. It does not have to be good and it does not have to be funny.
It has to be an artefact that claimed to say something in a language, and did
not.

## Before

Photograph it or transcribe it. Write down where you were and what day it was.
If you found it online rather than in the world, that is a different provenance,
not a worse one, and you write that down instead.

## In the bench

Two minutes each, in a fixed order: what it says, where it came from, what you
think produced it. Nobody argues about the third part yet. The room's only job
today is to find out whether the first two hold up, and they will not, for about
half of us.

## Afterwards

Your record goes into the shared corpus with your name on it. Anyone in the
course can use it, and anyone in the course can challenge its verification
level. That is the point of putting it there.
