The corpus goes on the wall and other people read it.

## Setup

Arrive an hour early. Cards go up with the provenance visible, not on the back
and not in a folder. A specimen whose verification level is hidden from the
viewer is presented as more certain than it is, which is the one failure this
course does not forgive.

## The visit

Members of the school walk through. They will not have been to any of the
lectures. Your wall text is the whole of what they get, and if it needs you
standing beside it, it is not finished.

## The four minutes

Then each of you walks the room through your own wall. Four minutes. Not a
defence — a reading. What is here, what is not, and which card you are least
sure about.

## Afterwards

The corpus stays. Next year's course starts from it, and every verification
level you wrote down becomes something a stranger inherits.
