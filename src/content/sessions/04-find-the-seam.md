A statistical system does not fail at a word. It fails at a join.

## Before

Bring one artefact you believe predates 2016: a product manual, a hotel notice,
a tourist brochure, a museum caption. Older signage is harder to find than it
sounds, and finding it is half the exercise.

## In the bench

Mark the join. Read the output aloud and stop at the first point where the
sentence stops being about the same thing as the source. That point is usually a
phrase boundary, and its position tells you roughly how long the units were that
the system was assembling.

Then argue about the date. Nothing on a sign says when it was translated, so you
are dating it from the shape of its errors, which is exactly the move the whole
course is built on and the first time you are asked to do it under pressure.
