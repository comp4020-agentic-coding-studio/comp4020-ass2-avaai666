Everything you have learned works on a broken surface. Today the surface is
smooth.

## The exercise

Take a translation produced by a current general-purpose model. It will read
well. Somewhere in it, something is wrong — a name, a register, a claim the
source did not make.

Now do what you did in week 2: reconstruct the mechanism from the output.

## What will happen

You will get further than you expect on the content and nowhere at all on the
mechanism. A wrong word in a lookup implicates an entry. A wrong claim in a
fluent paragraph implicates nothing you can point at.

Write down the exact point where the method stops working. That sentence is the
most valuable thing you will produce this semester, and it is the one the
lecture is about.
