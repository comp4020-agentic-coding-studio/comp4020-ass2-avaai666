Everyone in this room has heard that an early machine translation system turned
*the spirit is willing but the flesh is weak* into *the vodka is good but the
meat is rotten*. Today we find out who saw that happen.

## The method

Start from wherever you first met the story. Find the source it cites. Find that
source's source. Write each step down with its date. Continue until one of three
things happens: you reach a primary account, you reach a citation that does not
resolve, or you reach a source that cites nothing.

Most of you will hit the third. Write down where.

## Why this is a whole bench

Because the answer changes what the rest of the semester is allowed to say. If
the story has no source, then every page of this site that repeats it has to say
so, and the corpus needs a verification level that means *widely repeated,
unsourced* rather than quietly dropping it.

Failing to find a source is a finding. It goes in the record with the same
weight as a photograph.

## Afterwards

Compare chains across the room. Where two of you stopped at different points,
one of you found a step the other missed — that is the useful half hour.
