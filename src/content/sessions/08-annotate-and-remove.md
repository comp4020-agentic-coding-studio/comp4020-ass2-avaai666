Forty seconds of video, two subtitle tracks, and an argument about which one is
more honest.

## The exercise

Take a clip in a language you read. Subtitle it twice.

The first version keeps the seam: a bracketed note where a word does not carry
over, the original term left in when the substitute would lie, a line at the top
of the frame explaining a joke that cannot survive.

The second version is what a distributor would ship. No notes. No top-of-frame
text. Nothing that admits a translator was in the room.

## The argument

The second version reads better and is easier to watch, and everyone in the room
will prefer it. Then say what it can no longer tell you, and who decided that on
the viewer's behalf.

## Afterwards

A fansub is the only artefact in this course whose errors are deliberate. That
makes it the hardest thing to reconstruct backwards, because for once the
mechanism is a group of people with values rather than a table.
