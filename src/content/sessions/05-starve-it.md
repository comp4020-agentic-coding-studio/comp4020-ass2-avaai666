Today the room runs the experiment rather than reading the artefact.

## The protocol

1. Pick a language pair where one side has very little parallel text.
2. Feed it input with no content: repeated syllables, a string of the same
   character, a word with a typo in the middle.
3. Record every input, every output, the system, the direction, and the date.
   Screenshot the date.
4. Repeat one input four times over the session and see whether the answer moves.

## What to watch for

The interesting result is not a wrong translation. It is a well-formed sentence
about a subject that was not in the input, arriving with no hedging and no error
message.

## Afterwards

Your log is a dated artefact about a system that will have changed by the time
anyone reads it. That is why step 3 is in the protocol and not a suggestion. Six
months from now the log is the only evidence that this behaviour existed.
