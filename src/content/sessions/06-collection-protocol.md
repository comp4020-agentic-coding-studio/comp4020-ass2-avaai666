Until now you have been reading other people's records. From this week the
corpus is yours, and so is everything that goes wrong with it.

## The protocol

1. **Photograph the artefact straight on.** An angled photograph of a sign is a
   photograph of a wall.
2. **Capture the context in a second frame.** A menu item means something
   different on a menu than it does cropped.
3. **Write the date at the scene.** Not that evening. The number of specimens in
   any corpus that are dated to the day someone got home is embarrassing.
4. **Record the place at the granularity you would publish.** A suburb and a
   month is a provenance. A street address is somebody's workplace.
5. **Do not photograph the people under the sign.** If they are in frame and
   incidental, crop before the file is named.
6. **Ask, inside.** Shop interiors and menus are private property, and the
   answer is usually yes if the question is asked.

## Before you leave

Say the six steps out loud to the person next to you. The ones you cannot
remember are the ones you will skip on a wet afternoon in a car park.

## Afterwards

The break follows this week. Bring back four specimens, and at least one of them
should be something you would previously have walked past.
