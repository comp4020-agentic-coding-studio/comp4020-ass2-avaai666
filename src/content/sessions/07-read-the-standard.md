A standard is a document that says what a sign must say. A sign is a thing on a
wall. This bench is the gap.

## Before

Bring a clause. Any published guidance that prescribes wording for public
signage will do, in any language, and the more specific it is about individual
signs the better.

## In the bench

Put the clause and the artefact next to each other and answer three questions in
order:

Who was obliged? Who actually produced the sign? What sat between them — a
contractor, a sign shop, a procurement form, an email?

The third question is the one that pays. In the Swansea case the entire
mechanism is an email nobody read, and no translation system was involved at any
point.

want to argue about, change it; the deadline is for the argument, not for the
first thing you picked.
