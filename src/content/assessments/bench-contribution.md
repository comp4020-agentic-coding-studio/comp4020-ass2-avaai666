## What this is for

A bench is not a seminar and it does not work if people arrive empty-handed. The
mark exists because the corpus is shared: your record becomes somebody else's
evidence, and the only quality control it has is the room.

## What counts

Bringing an artefact. Saying that somebody's `primary` looks like a `secondary`
to you, and why. Accepting the same about your own without treating it as a
loss.

## What does not

Talking. Agreeing warmly. Attendance on its own — being present at twelve
benches with nothing on the table is worth roughly nothing here, and that is
stated plainly so nobody discovers it in week 12.

## If you cannot attend

Tell the convenor before the bench, and submit the artefact you would have
brought. Benches cannot be made up, because the thing being marked is the effect
you had on other people's records, and that does not happen afterwards.
