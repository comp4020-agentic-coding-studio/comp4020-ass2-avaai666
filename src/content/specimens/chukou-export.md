<span lang="zh-Hans">出口</span> means *exit*. It also means *export*. The two
senses are unrelated in English and inseparable in the written form, and a door
in an airport is labelled with one of them.

<blockquote data-specimen>
  <p><span lang="zh-Hans">出口</span> &nbsp; EXPORT</p>
</blockquote>

## Why it opens the course

Nothing here is broken. No character was merged, no statistical model was
trained, no network hallucinated. A word had two senses, something chose the
wrong one, and the something could have been a dictionary, a phrase book, a
contractor, or a person in a hurry.

That is the condition every other specimen in this corpus is a variation on, and
it is why week 1 is about establishing the claim rather than diagnosing the
fault. Until you can say what this sign promised, you cannot say it failed.

## What this record does not establish

Which artefact. Signs of this kind are photographed constantly and reproduced
without provenance, and the course has not recorded one in the field. It sits at
`secondary` until somebody in this room takes the photograph.
