A council in Swansea needed a bilingual sign prohibiting heavy goods vehicles
from a residential road. The English was written, the text was emailed for
translation, a reply came back, and the reply was printed on the sign and
installed.

The Welsh on the road said, in substance:

<blockquote data-specimen>
  <p>I am not in the office at the moment. Send any work to be translated.</p>
</blockquote>

## The mechanism is not a translation system

There is no dictionary here, no phrase table, no model. There is an email
address, an automatic reply, and a production process in which no step required
anybody to read the output in the language it was written in.

This is the only specimen in the corpus whose reconstruction terminates in an
office procedure, and it is the one that most reliably changes how students read
the others. A great many public translation errors are not translation errors.

## Why it is primary

The sign was photographed installed, and the photograph was published with the
report. The artefact and the record of the artefact are the same object, which
is the strongest provenance anything in this corpus has.
