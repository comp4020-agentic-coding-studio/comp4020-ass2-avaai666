A rule-based system breaks in a way that looks broken. A statistical one breaks
in a way that looks like English for six words and then turns.

The reason is where the text came from. Parliamentary proceedings and
legislative records were among the few large, sentence-aligned bilingual corpora
available, so the systems learned the register of committee minutes and applied
it to menus.

## Outline

- parallel corpora: what was available, and what that made the systems good at
- the phrase table as a suspect with a memory but no understanding
- locating a join, and what its position tells you about unit length
- dating an artefact from the shape of its errors
