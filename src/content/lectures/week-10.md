Every specimen so far was found. This week we make them.

Back-translation is old and mostly used for quality checking, which is not what
we are doing with it. Chain a translation out and back enough times and the text
stops changing. What it settles into is not a translation of your sentence. It is
the system's preferred output for its own previous output, which makes the fixed
point a description of the system rather than of the source.

That is a measurement, taken from outside, of something nobody will let us open.

## Outline

- back-translation, and why it is a bad quality check and a good probe
- convergence: where the fixed point usually sits and why
- reading register, length and vocabulary off a fixed point
- the limits: what a probe from outside can never establish
