Simplification merged <span lang="zh-Hans">乾</span> (*gān*, dry) and
<span lang="zh-Hans">幹</span> (*gàn*, to do, and in some registers to do to
somebody) into the single form <span lang="zh-Hans">干</span>. A lookup keyed on
that form inherits every sense at once and has no context with which to choose.

What comes out of that table is printed on supermarket aisles and restaurant
menus, and it is a legible description of the table. The senses are there, in an
order, and the order is a decision somebody made about frequency in a corpus
that was not this one.
