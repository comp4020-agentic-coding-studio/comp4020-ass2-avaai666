A commercial subtitle removes the seam. It is paid to. The viewer is not
supposed to notice that a decision was made, and the highest praise for the work
is that nobody thought about it.

Volunteer subtitling communities frequently do the opposite. A bracketed note in
the middle of a line, a term left untranslated because the substitute would lie,
a paragraph at the top of the frame explaining a joke that cannot survive the
crossing: these are not failures of craft. They are an audience that has asked
to see the working.

## Outline

- the fansub as an annotated edition rather than a rough one
- what a translator's note does to the viewer's relationship with the text
- reconstructing a community's values from what it refuses to smooth
- the hardest reconstruction in the course: a mechanism made of people
