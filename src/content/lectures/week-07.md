Some errors are not made by translators. They are made by procurement.

China's national guidance on English in public service areas prescribes the
wording of thousands of individual signs, which turns a translation question
into a compliance question and moves the error from the translator to whoever
did or did not read the clause.

Then there is the Welsh road sign a Swansea council put up in 2008, where the
English was emailed to a translator and the Welsh that came back — and went onto
the sign, and onto the road — was an out-of-office auto-reply. No translation
system was involved. The mechanism was an inbox.
